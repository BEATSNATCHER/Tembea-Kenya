FAQ

What is the best time to visit Kenya?

The best time to visit Kenya is during the dry season, which typically runs from June to October.

What are some must-see destinations in Kenya?

Some must-see destinations in Kenya include the Maasai Mara National Reserve, Amboseli National Park, and Lake Nakuru National Park.

Is it safe to travel to Kenya?

Kenya is generally safe for tourists, but it's important to stay informed about local conditions and follow travel advisories.
